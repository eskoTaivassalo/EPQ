## 🎯 1. VISION & OBJECTIVES

### Core Vision

**What is the main purpose of the app?**
Connecting parents and teachers. Later expanded to cover other social sectors such as doctors, therapists, etc.

**What problem does the app solve? Why don't current solutions (e.g., Facebook groups) work?**
Reliability: Users log in and authenticate strongly (Google authentication), which adds security and better privacy compared to Facebook groups.

**Who are the main users?**
Main users are parents and teachers.

### Business Objectives

**Target user base in 6-12 months:**
People who have money to pay for finding necessary help for children through the platform.

**Is this a paid or free app?**
Paid app.

**How does monetization work?**
